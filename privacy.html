Privacy Policy

El Nudo Invisible Publisher (the "App") · Last updated: October 2026

What the App is

The App is a private tool operated by a single individual, the owner of the TikTok account @elnudoinvisibledoc. It is used only to upload that owner's own educational videos to that same account through the TikTok Content Posting API. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

Data we access

Through TikTok Login Kit the App receives an access token for the owner's own account, together with basic profile information (display name and avatar). Videos are uploaded only when the owner starts an upload.

How data is stored and used

  • The access token and client credentials are stored locally on the owner's computer, in a protected folder that is not published or shared.
  • The data is used only to upload the owner's videos to the owner's account.
  • We do not sell, rent, share or disclose any data to third parties, and we do not use it for advertising or analytics.
  • We do not collect data from any other TikTok user.

Retention and deletion

The access token is kept only while it is needed. The owner can revoke the App's access at any time in TikTok under Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions, and can delete the stored token from the local computer.

Contact

Questions: via the TikTok account @elnudoinvisibledoc

Política de privacidad

Esta herramienta privada la opera una sola persona, titular de la cuenta de TikTok @elnudoinvisibledoc, y solo sirve para subir sus propios videos educativos a esa misma cuenta mediante la API de publicación de contenido de TikTok. No se ofrece al público.

La herramienta recibe un token de acceso a la cuenta del titular y datos básicos de su perfil. El token y las credenciales se guardan solo en el equipo del titular, en una carpeta protegida. No se venden ni se comparten datos con terceros, no se usan para publicidad ni analítica y no se recopilan datos de otros usuarios. El titular puede revocar el acceso en TikTok (Configuración y privacidad → Seguridad → Administrar permisos de apps) y borrar el token local en cualquier momento.

Contacto: a través de la cuenta de TikTok @elnudoinvisibledoc
